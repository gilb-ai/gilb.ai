Therblig · How it works

Two streams. One map of the work.

Therblig runs AI interviews and screen capture side by side, then merges them. Every finding links what a person said to what they actually did.

Request a pilot

Words and actions, merged.

Each stream covers the blind spot of the other.

Stream A

AI interviews

Adaptive voice or text conversations with each participant: goals, pain points, exceptions, and why the work is done this way.

Stream B

Desktop context

A consent-based recorder on agreed apps: clicks, keystrokes, app switches and repeated sequences. The work behind the work.

↓ ↓

Task map with evidence

Stated tasks are matched to observed sequences. Gaps between the two become questions for follow-up interviews.

From scope to skills.

Timelines depend on team size and the apps in scope. We agree them before the study starts.

  1. Define the study

    Set the goal, the teams, required questions, topics to avoid, and the apps and period for capture.

    You and Gilb

  2. Agree consent and data rules

    Participant notice and opt-in, retention, anonymisation and storage location. Employee representatives review where required.

    You and the client

  3. Interview the team

    Each participant talks to the AI interviewer at a time that suits them. Interviews can be anonymous.

    Stream A

  4. Capture on-screen work

    Participants run the recorder on agreed apps and can pause it any time. Sensitive content is filtered.

    Stream B

  5. Merge and review

    We build the task map, rank opportunities and draft agent skills. People review every proposal before it is used.

    Gilb

  6. Hand off to delivery

    You present the findings. Approved skills can be deployed and operated on Company Brain.

    You, then Company Brain

Questions

Do participants need to install anything?

Interviews run in the browser. Screen capture uses a recorder installed on agreed devices for the study period only.

Can we run only interviews or only capture?

Yes. The merged view gives the strongest evidence, but each stream can run on its own.

Who sees the raw recordings?

Access is defined in the study scope. Leadership sees findings and anonymised excerpts, not raw screen feeds.

Can we brand the study for our client?

Yes. Delivery partners can run Therblig white-label or co-branded. See the partner program.
