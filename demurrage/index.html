What we take on

The claim work.
From records to recovery.

A focused service for shipowners, operators and disponent owners with unpaid vessel-demurrage claims. Start with up to three claims and agree the work before we begin.

01

Collect and organise the evidence

Bring together the records you authorise us to use. Match emails and attachments to the voyage, identify missing documents, and keep each event linked to its source.

02

Reconstruct the timeline and calculation

Review the charter party and amendments against the port timeline. Check when laytime starts, which interruptions count, and how the agreed demurrage rate applies. Flag conflicts and assumptions for review.

03

Prepare and follow up the claim

Prepare the calculation and supporting documents. With your authority, submit the claim, follow up with the counterparty and address requests for evidence. Track contractual submission deadlines.

04

Keep you in control through settlement

Maintain a record of correspondence, disputed items and proposed settlements. You approve settlement terms. Match payments to the agreed claims before calculating our fee.

AI can assist with document organisation and extraction. Contract interpretation, disputed facts and claim calculations require human review. We agree the review arrangements before accepting a claim.

What goes into the claim

The context behind
every hour claimed.

A timestamp alone is not enough. We connect what happened with the contract terms and the documents that support it.

Records we work from

  • Charter party, fixture recap and amendments
  • Statement of Facts and port time sheets
  • Notice of Readiness and evidence of receipt
  • Captain, agent, terminal and charterer correspondence
  • Available weather, stoppage and operational records
  • Existing calculations, invoices and payment history

Points we check against the contract

  • Arrival, readiness and the start of laytime
  • Loading and discharge periods
  • Weather, holidays and other agreed exceptions
  • Interruptions, shifting and disputed periods
  • Allowed time, applicable rates and previous payments
  • Notice requirements and claim submission deadlines

Treatment depends on the contract and available evidence. Missing records and conflicting accounts are flagged, not filled in with assumptions.

Before we start

A clear scope. No surprises.

Which claims are a fit?

The pilot focuses on unpaid vessel-demurrage claims under charter parties, where you are the claimant or authorised to act for the claimant. We first assess the documents, contractual deadlines, disputed issues and payment prospects. Not every claim will be accepted. Container demurrage and detention refunds are outside this pilot.

Do you guarantee recovery or advance the money?

No. Recovery depends on the evidence, contract, dispute and counterparty’s ability to pay. The pilot does not include advances, debt purchase or insurance.

Can you work with our existing team and software?

Yes. The proposed pilot starts from agreed document exports and correspondence. You do not need to replace your voyage management system. Access, confidentiality and document transfer arrangements are agreed before files are shared.

Who contacts the counterparty and approves settlement?

We agree authority and communication arrangements in writing before contacting anyone on your behalf. You retain approval of settlement terms. Legal proceedings and external legal costs require separate approval.

What if we have already received part of the payment?

We record the starting balance and previous receipts in the engagement. Our fee applies only to cash recovered after the engagement starts on the claims expressly included. Partial payments and the invoicing schedule are defined in the agreement.

What do you need for the first conversation?

A short overview of up to three claims: approximate amounts, age, current status, any known deadlines and the documents available. An anonymised summary is enough to discuss fit. Please do not send sensitive documents through the general enquiry form.
