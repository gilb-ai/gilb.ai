Questions

What does the SLA cover?

Service targets, support hours, incident priorities, response times and escalation. The exact terms are agreed before launch.

Does Company Brain replace our systems of record?

No. The CRM and other authoritative systems stay the source of truth. Company Brain gives the team access to their connected records and history.

How much historical data can we access?

It depends on what the source systems retain and allow us to connect. We agree the date range and known gaps before implementation. Deleted history cannot be assumed recoverable.

Where will the system run?

We agree the environment and operating responsibilities during scoping, based on existing systems, access rules and infrastructure needs.

Can we offer Company Brain to our clients under our brand?

Yes. Delivery partners can offer it white-label or co-branded. Terms are agreed when you join the partner program.

How do we get started?

Bring one workflow, the systems it depends on, and a few questions that need historical context. We map the deployment scope from there.
