Privacy notice

Last updated: 31 October 2026

What this covers

This notice explains how Gilb handles the personal details you send through the request forms on gilb.ai. It does not cover client data processed in Therblig or Company Brain engagements; those terms are agreed in each client or partner contract.

What we collect

When you send a request, we receive what you type into the form:

  • your name;
  • your company or firm size;
  • the description of what you need;
  • your email address;
  • your WhatsApp number, if you choose to give it;
  • the page the request came from and a technical request ID used to avoid saving the same request twice.

The site does not use advertising or analytics cookies. The animated plate on the home page runs only in your browser; nothing about your cursor or touch movements is sent to us.

Why we use it

We use these details only to reply to your request, to discuss a possible partnership or engagement, and to keep a record of that conversation. We do not sell your details or use them for unrelated marketing.

Where it is stored

Requests are delivered through a Google Apps Script web app and stored in our Google Workspace account. Google acts as our service provider for this storage.

How long we keep it

We keep a request for as long as the conversation it started is active, and for up to 24 months afterwards, unless you ask us to delete it sooner.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the details you sent, or to stop contacting you. Reply to any email from us or write to the address we used to answer your request, and we will act on it.

Changes

If we change how we handle these details, we will update this page and the date above.
