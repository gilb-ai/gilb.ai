PARTNER QUESTIONS

The essentials.

Who is the program for?

Management and strategy consultancies, systems integrators, AI and automation agencies, and transformation offices inside companies with 300+ people.

Do we need technical staff?

No. We run the technology and train your team to scope studies and present results.

Can our client see the Gilb brand?

Only if you want. White-label and co-branded delivery are both possible; the scope depends on the partnership.

How does the commercial model work?

Delivery partners set their own client price and buy the tools from us at partner terms. Referral partners earn a fee per qualified client. Terms are agreed when you join.
