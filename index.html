Questions partners ask

Who is the partner program for?

Management and strategy consultancies, systems integrators, AI and automation agencies, and transformation offices inside companies with 300+ people.

Do we need technical staff?

No. We run the technology and train your team to scope studies and present results.

Will the client see the Gilb brand?

Only if you want. White label and co branded delivery are both possible; the scope depends on the partnership.

How does the commercial model work?

Delivery partners set their own client price and buy the tools at partner terms. Referral partners earn a fee per qualified client. Terms are shared after an intro call and agreed when you join.

What does the first engagement look like?

One client and one piece of work: a Therblig study or a Company Brain deployment. Scope, consent and data rules are agreed before anything starts.

Is screen capture a form of employee surveillance?

No. People opt in, capture covers only agreed apps and time, sensitive content is filtered out, and managers see the task map and opportunities, never a live view of someone’s screen. If the goal is productivity policing, Therblig is the wrong tool.

Does Company Brain replace the client’s systems?

No. The CRM and other systems of record stay the source of truth. Company Brain connects their history and makes it usable for people and agents.

Who keeps the system running after launch?

We do. Service targets, support hours, incident response and escalation are agreed in an SLA before launch.
