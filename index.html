BEFORE WE START

The essentials.

What does the SLA cover?

The service-level agreement defines the commitments for your deployment: service targets, support hours, incident priorities, response times, and escalation. The exact terms are agreed before launch.

Does Company Brain replace our systems of record?

No. Your CRM and other authoritative systems remain the source of truth. Company Brain gives your team access to their connected records and available history through a common interface.

How much historical data can we access?

Coverage depends on what your source systems retain and allow us to connect. We agree on the date range, available versions, and gaps before implementation. Missing or deleted history cannot be assumed recoverable.

Where will the system run?

We agree on the deployment environment and operating responsibilities during scoping, based on your existing systems, access requirements, and infrastructure needs.

How do you handle access to company data?

We define users, permissions, and data-handling requirements before connecting sources. Storage, retention, and any compliance requirements are addressed in the deployment scope.

How do we get started?

Bring one workflow, the systems it depends on, and a few questions that need historical context. We will map the deployment scope and the operational commitments it requires.
