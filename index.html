The method is a century old. The work moved to screens.

The Gilbreths fixed small lights to workers’ hands and photographed every motion. Therblig does the same for clicks, keystrokes and app switches, and adds the interview that explains why.

1910s

Chronocyclegraphs: light traces of hand motion, split into elementary units called therbligs.

Now

Screen traces and AI interviews, merged into a task map that agents can act on.
